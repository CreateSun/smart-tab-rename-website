Plain-language privacy

Your tab data stays
in your browser.

Tab Rename was built to organize tabs — not to observe the people using them.

What we collect

Tab Rename does not collect, transmit, sell or share tab titles, URLs, rules or settings. It contains no analytics, advertising SDKs or remote code.

What stays on your device

Permanent rules and paused sites live in Chrome local storage. Names held for a tab session live in Chrome session storage. A page-only name lives only in the current page’s extension memory.

Why optional site access exists

If you choose to enable it, optional access lets permanent rules apply after a page navigates. It is used only to read and update the current tab title on sites you authorize. You can remove this access in Chrome at any time.

Your controls

You can edit, disable, export or delete rules from the extension’s management page. Uninstalling removes local extension data and may open an optional Tally-hosted feedback page. Its URL does not include your tab names, visited URLs or rules; you can close it without submitting anything.
